How to play

Dig channels

Click or tap soil to carve a channel. Water flows through open cells.

Packed clay

Banded clay costs 2 digs. Watch your budget.

Foul pockets

Spiked ochre pockets foul any water that touches them. Route around.

The spring

Water flows down, then spreads sideways. It can never climb.

The well

Fill the glowing well with clean water before the spring runs dry.
